YGO Scanner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

YGO Scanner ("the app") scans trading cards with your phone's camera, reads the card's set code or passcode, and shows you the card and links to it on Cardmarket. This policy explains what data the app uses. In short: we don't collect, store or sell any personal data.

Camera

The app asks for camera access so you can scan cards. Camera images are processed only on your device to read the text printed on the card. Images and video are never uploaded, saved or shared.

Photos you choose

If you use "Photo", the picture you select is read on your device in the same way and is not uploaded or kept.

Data sent to other services

  • YGOPRODeck (db.ygoprodeck.com): the card code or card name that was read or typed is sent to look up the card details and image. No personal information is sent.
  • The app's website (zetsuu123.github.io), hosted by GitHub Pages: the app's pages and its card-code-to-Cardmarket index are loaded from there.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): the text-recognition engine (Tesseract.js) is downloaded from there.
  • Cardmarket: only when you tap a Cardmarket link, it opens in your browser or the Cardmarket app, under Cardmarket's own privacy policy.

Like any website, these services may receive technical data such as your IP address as part of normal network requests. The app itself does not add identifiers, use analytics, or show ads.

Data stored on your device

Your recent scans and settings (Cardmarket language, seller country) are saved only on your device. You can delete the history with "Clear" in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Zetsuu123/zetsuu123.github.io.
