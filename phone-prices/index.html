Phone Prices

Automatic price comparison works in the Android app. In a browser this page can only link you to each store's search.

The cheapest smartphone, in one search

Type a model and the app checks Skroutz, Public, Kotsovolos, Plaisio and Germanos at the same time, then shows the lowest price first.

  • The same phone in different colours counts as one model.
  • Cases, chargers and other accessories are left out.
  • Out-of-stock and refurbished phones are hidden unless you turn them on in .
