Phone Prices – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Phone Prices ("the app") searches smartphone prices on Skroutz, Public, Kotsovolos, Plaisio and Germanos and shows you the cheapest. In short: we don't collect, store or sell any personal data.

How searches work

When you search, the app opens each store's own search page on your phone, the same way your browser would, and reads the product names and prices shown there. Your search words go directly from your phone to those stores. Nothing goes through a server of ours, because there isn't one.

Data the stores receive

Each store you search receives what any visitor's browser sends: your search words, your IP address, your device's browser information, and the cookies that store set on earlier visits. The stores' own privacy policies apply to that. You can delete those cookies in the app under Filters → Clear store cookies.

Data kept on your phone

Your recent searches, your filter settings and the search addresses the app learned for each store are kept only in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

The app's website

The app's pages are loaded from zetsuu123.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data such as IP addresses (see GitHub's privacy statement).
